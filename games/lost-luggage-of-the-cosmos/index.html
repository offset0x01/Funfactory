Lost Luggage of the Cosmos

You are a space courier in fuzzy slippers. Deliver the mystery parcel across a junkyard planet, a malfunctioning cruise ship and an asteroid that will not stop talking.

Move
Arrow keys or A / D. On iPad: tilt, or the arrow buttons.
Jump
Space, Up or W. On iPad: the JUMP button.
Fizzy Jet
Grab the green can, then press jump again in mid-air for a burp-powered double jump.
Stomp
Land on robots and space jellies. Touching them from the side costs a slipper (a life).
Purple zones
Gravity flips! Walk on the ceiling.
Pause
P or Esc. M turns sound on or off. F toggles full screen.
