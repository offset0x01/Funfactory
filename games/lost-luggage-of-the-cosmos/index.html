Lost Luggage of the Cosmos

You are a space courier in fuzzy slippers. Deliver the mystery parcel across thirteen stops: a junkyard planet, a wobbly cruise ship, a talking asteroid, an icy comet, a hot sauce moon and the Galactic Lost & Found, where the Baggage Beast is waiting. Then follow the Star Compass into the Cosmic Vault.

Move
Arrow keys or A / D. On iPad: tilt, or the arrow buttons.
Jump
Space, Up or W. On iPad: the JUMP button.
Fizzy Jet
Grab the green can, then press jump again in mid-air for a burp-powered double jump.
Stomp
Land on robots, jellies, hoppers and drones. Touching them from the side costs a slipper (a life).
Ouch!
Spiky snails and fire flares can't be stomped: jump over them. Stay out of the hot sauce.
Power-ups
Blue bubble: a shield that blocks one hit. Rainbow star: Mega Fuzz, 8 seconds of smashing through everything.
Coins
Every bolt pays 3 coins into your bank. Collect 150 coins in a run and you earn an extra slipper, on the spot!
Shop
Spend saved coins on skins, hats, fun items and next-run power-ups. Look for hidden spare slippers on high shelves!
Purple zones
Gravity flips! Walk on the ceiling.
Pause
P or Esc. M turns sound on or off. F toggles full screen.
