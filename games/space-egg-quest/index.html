Space Egg Quest

Your ship landed on Planet Wobblor. Find the 8 glowing alien eggs. Each one hatches into a little buddy who follows you and zaps the Gloomlings!

Find every egg and MEGA GLOOM comes down from the sky. Beat it with your buddies to win.

Keyboard: W / Up arrow to walk, S / Down to back up, Left / Right or A / D to turn, Q / E to step sideways, Space to blast, drag the mouse to look. P pauses, M mutes.
Touch: the stick walks and turns, drag anywhere to look, the big button blasts.

Follow the green arrow at the top: it points to the nearest egg.
