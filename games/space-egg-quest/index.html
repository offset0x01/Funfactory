Space Egg Quest

Your ship landed on Planet Wobblor, overrun by monsters. Beat them: some drop alien eggs! Find 20 eggs. Each one hatches into a little buddy who follows you and helps a bit.

Four caves (new ones every game) hide the Big Laser, the Grenade Launcher, the Sniper and a Jet-Pack. Careful: the caves are trapped — twice as many traps as before! Bear traps, trip-wires, swinging axes, pop-up spikes, sweeping lasers, rolling boulders and crumbling ceilings. Jump the low ones, time the rest.

Keyboard: W / S walk, A / D or Left / Right turn, Up / Down look up and down, Q / E step sideways, Space fires, J or Shift jumps, 1 to 5 or X switch weapons (5 is the Jet-Pack: hold Space to fly — it drinks fuel, and every monster you beat refills a good chunk), drag the mouse to look. P pauses, M mutes.
Touch: the stick walks and turns, drag anywhere to look around and up and down, FIRE, JUMP, and SWAP for weapons (hold FIRE with the Jet-Pack to fly).

No aim help: shots go exactly where the crosshair points. The egg sensor at the top points to a dropped egg within 40 m.
