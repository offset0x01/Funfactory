Space Egg Quest

Your ship landed on Planet Wobblor. Explore the hills, mountains and alien forests to find 10 alien eggs. Two are hidden down in underground dungeons! Each egg hatches into a little buddy who follows you and zaps monsters.

Watch out for Gloomlings, dashing Zapbugs, goo-spitting mushrooms and tough Rockbacks. Find every egg and MEGA GLOOM comes down from the sky. It can teleport and fly!

Keyboard: W / Up arrow to walk, S / Down to back up, Left / Right or A / D to turn, Q / E to step sideways, Space to blast, drag the mouse to look. P pauses, M mutes.
Touch: the stick walks and turns, drag anywhere to look, the big button blasts.

Your egg sensor at the top points to an egg once you get within 40 m. Explore to find them!
