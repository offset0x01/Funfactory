Threeble

A word game inside a cube! Words can run left-right, up-down or front-back. Highest score when the tiles run out wins.

  • Spin the cube with one finger. Pinch to zoom, twist two fingers to turn it.
  • Tap a tile to build a word from it. Add your tiles on either side, then Swing the word until it fits. A Space lets your word cross a tile already on the board.
  • Drag the Peel slider down to peel layers off the front and see inside.
  • Glass blocks are bonus squares: 2× or 3× the letter (blue) or the whole word (pink, red). Use all 7 tiles for +50.

Arrows spin, Q/E twist, + and − zoom, [ and ] peel, L locks the peel. In the builder: 1–7 add a tile, Tab picks the side, G adds a space, Backspace takes one back, S swings, Enter or Space plays, C closes. H hint, X swap all. P or Esc pauses.
