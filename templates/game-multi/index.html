Paddle Party

Up to 4 players, one on each side. Don't let the ball past your paddle: every miss costs a heart. Last one standing wins.

Drag along your side to move your paddle, or use the arrow keys. P pauses.

How many players?

Lay the phone or tablet flat in the middle. Each player drags along their own side. On one keyboard: arrows, A D, T G and I K.
